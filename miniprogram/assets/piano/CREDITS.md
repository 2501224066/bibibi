**Salamander Grand Piano** by **Alexander Holm**, distributed by Tone.js.

Included samples: C3, Fs3, C4, Fs4, C5, Fs5, C6.
Modifications: converted from MP3 to mono PCM16 WAV at 22,050 Hz, trimmed the
lead-in and length to three seconds, normalized peaks to 80%, and added a
180 ms end fade. Intermediate notes are pitch-shifted during playback.

Reproduce with `python3 scripts/prepare-piano.py` (macOS afconvert required).

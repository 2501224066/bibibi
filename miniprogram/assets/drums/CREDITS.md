Pearl Master Studio Pack 1 by **enoe**, distributed by the Oramics Sampled collection.

Included recordings: snare-01, tom-01, hihat-closed, hihat-open, crash-01, ride-01,
tom-02, tom-03, kick-01, snare-02, crash-02, splash-01 (.wav).

Modifications: downmixed stereo to mono, peak-normalized to 85%, and stored as
standard 44-byte-header mono PCM16 WAV files at 44,100 Hz. No synthetic drum substitution.
The preparation script is `scripts/prepare-drums.py`. These credits and the
license notice must accompany redistribution. No endorsement is implied.
